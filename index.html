The why behind anything, drawn in minutes.

Pen & Why takes one story a week — a headline, a money mystery, a science question or a how-to — and explains it with simple hand-drawn sketches in about five minutes.

Every video is researched from several sources, which are listed in its description. No hype, no jargon, just the why.
